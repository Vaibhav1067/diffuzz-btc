- Reviewers of the project itself (grant reviewers, security researchers) checking the scale,

They read the results, they do not operate the fuzzer from this page.

## Product Purpose

diffuzz-btc feeds identical (transaction, spent outputs, input index, consensus flags) cases to
independent Bitcoin consensus implementations and reports any disagreement on accept/reject.
The results page makes a run's evidence readable at a glance: how much was executed, how fast,
how the verdicts split, and that every disagreement is either explained or absent.

## Positioning

Differential testing across four script interpreters (Core 26.0, btcd, NBitcoin, libbitcoin
v4) plus a sighash/serialization differential, with every case reproducible from one JSON file
and pinned versions. The claim is "zero unexplained divergences over N cases", not "bug found".

## Operating Context

Results come from `diffuzz fuzz --summary`, `scripts/run_overnight.sh` (merged `summary.json`),
`diffuzz replay` and `diffuzz aux`. Readers open the page from a repository checkout, a CI
artifact or a link in a write-up (`docs/WRITEUP.md`).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Only `ok` (accept/reject) is compared; error classes are informational.
- Verdict classes: `AGREE_OK`, `AGREE_FAIL`, `KNOWN` (allowlisted), `HARD_DIVERGE`, `CRASH`,
  `TIMEOUT`, `UNDECIDED`.
- **Responsible disclosure is a hard rule.** The page must never contain private findings,
  case contents of divergences, or names/details of private allowlist entries. Aggregate
  `KNOWN` counts may appear; private entry ids must not.
- Rates are per machine and must be labelled with the machine and date.

## Evidence on Hand

Run summaries in `out/` (git-ignored, regenerated per run): `out/bench/*.json`,
`out/run-*/summary.json`. Published figures are in `docs/WRITEUP.md` and
`docs/corpus-results.md`. No testimonials, users or deployments exist; none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Honest numbers first: show exactly what ran, with seed, duration and versions.
2. Disagreement is the signal: the divergence status must be unmissable, success or not.
3. Reproducible: every figure traces back to a command and a pinned version.
4. Disclosure-safe by construction: private data cannot reach the page.
